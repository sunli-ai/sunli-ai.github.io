# About Me
Dr. Sun, Li is currently an Associate Professor at **Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications**, Beijing, China. Before joining BUPT, I have been an assistant professor (2021-2023), and associate professor (2024-2025) at North China Electric Power University (NCEPU), Beijing.

My research areas are **foundation models** and **machine learning with special attentions to Riemannian geometry**. I have published over 60 refereed top conference and journal papers, including ICML, NeurIPS, KDD, WWW, AAAI, IJCAI, WSDM, ICDM, CIKM, TOIS, TKDE, TWEB, TIST, TNSE, etc., amassing over 2200 citations on Google Scholar in the past 5 years. I routinely served as program committee member of flagship conferences and reviewer of IEEE/ACM transactions. He is the recipient of ACM CIKM 2022 best paper winners. I have been on the organization committee of IEEE DSS’24 and IEEE SocialCom’23. I have been a Guest Editor for the Springer JMLC and currently serve as a Guest Editor of IEEE TKDE Special Issue on Graph Foundation Models.
